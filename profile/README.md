## 🌟 Vision

Systole is an **artist-first music streaming platform** built on transparency, fairness, and creativity.  
We believe music should beat with the pulse of its creators - not be buried under opaque algorithms that favour the top artists or unfair payouts where we take all the money.  

Our mission: **empower artists, delight listeners, and keep the rhythm of music alive.**

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Systole’s design is inspired by **bold, heart-driven visuals** and our Figma prototypes.  
Every screen is crafted to feel alive, like a pulse - immersive, emotional, and artist-centric.

- **Home Dashboard**: A welcoming hub with curated albums and playlists.  
- **Playlists**: Artist mixes and algorithm-driven recommendations that adapt to your tastes.  
- **Now Playing**: Clean, immersive playback with **lyrics integration** and **art-forward album displays**.  
- **Artist Profiles**: Strong visual identity, giving creators the spotlight they deserve.  

---

## 🚀 Features

- ❤️ **Artist-first streaming** - transparent payouts, no hidden cuts.  
- 🎧 **Algorithm-based playlists** - personalized mixes that evolve with your listening habits.  
- 🖼 **Immersive UI** - bold visuals, lyric sync, and artwork-forward design.  
- 🔍 **Metadata integrity** - every track, lyric, and timestamp preserved with precision.  
- 🔒 **Secure streaming** - tokenized endpoints with range support.  
- 🤝 **Community-driven** - artists and developers shape the platform together.
- 🎵 **High-quality** - users can enjoy Lossless quality, rivalling popular streaming services, for free.

---

## 📐 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Svelte + TypeScript (modular, reactive, artist-first UI)  
- **Backend**: Node.js / PHP hybrid with secure streaming endpoints  
- **Data Integrity**: Metadata-preserving pipelines 
- **Infrastructure**: Cross-platform, PC friendly, with Mobile apps coming soon!  
- **Design**: Figma-driven, with iterative artist feedback loops
